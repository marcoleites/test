    Score breakdown

    Heuristic desk tool, not investment advice. Scoring is a rules-based approximation (moving-average trend, swing highs, ATR-based risk:reward) meant to mirror a discretionary "professional trade score" checklist — it is not a substitute for your own analysis. Data comes from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints (weekly bars, 2y; daily bars, 1y). Sector strength uses the SPDR sector ETF matching the ticker's GICS sector; market strength uses SPY. As with the after-hours dashboard, running the included local backend (FIRECRAWL_API_KEY=fc-... node server.js) makes fetches faster and more reliable — without it, the page falls back to public CORS proxies, and every failed route is listed in the error message.
